Memory, HBM & DRAM

The largest divergence in the market: contract prices rising double digits a quarter while the equities fall.

Stage · Accelerating fundamentally — de-rating in price

High-bandwidth memory is the component that determines how fast an accelerator can be fed data, which makes it one of the genuine physical constraints on AI deployment rather than a commodity input. All 2026 HBM supply is reported sold out, DRAM contract prices have risen sharply for three consecutive quarters, and Samsung has pushed for third-quarter increases of up to 20% — a cumulative year-to-date ASP move of roughly 340%. Against that, Samsung and SK hynix fell more than 15% in a single session in late July and Micron trades at 5.3x forward earnings, which makes this the sharpest fundamental-versus-price divergence in our coverage.

Be honest about lateness

Where we are in the lifecycle

Be honest about lateness. Most money in thematic investing is lost by arriving at "Crowded."

Accelerating on fundamentals; de-rating on price. These are usually the same phase and here they are not, which is the whole point of the theme.

Evidence for this staging: contract pricing is still rising sequentially; HBM is sold out for the year; capacity is being reallocated from commodity DRAM toward HBM and server DRAM, which paradoxically tightens the commodity market further. Meanwhile the equities trade at trough multiples — Micron at 5.3x forward — which is the market's explicit statement that these earnings are the peak. Both things are true at once and only one of them can persist.

Second- and third-order beneficiaries

This is where the non-obvious money is. Who supplies the supplier?

Memory equipment is the cleanest second-order expression and it is under-owned. HBM requires stacking and through-silicon-via formation, which adds process steps relative to conventional DRAM. That means tool intensity per wafer rises — Lam Research participates in the cycle through content growth as well as volume, which is a better economic position than simply riding capacity additions.

Third-order: the cost of memory is becoming a margin variable for everyone who buys it. If DRAM ASPs are up ~340% year-to-date, every server OEM, every phone maker and every hyperscaler has a materially higher bill of materials. That has not been widely modelled, and it will show up first in the gross margins of whoever has the least pricing power.

What the market is rewarding right now

Nothing. That is the honest answer and it is unusual enough to be worth stating plainly. The market is not currently rewarding pricing power, sold-out capacity, margin expansion or earnings growth in this theme — all four are present and the equities are falling. The market is pricing a cycle turn that the data does not yet show. Either it is forecasting better than the data (memory investors have often done exactly that) or this is forced de-risking spilling from the broader AI complex. We think it is materially the second; the evidence is that three separately-listed companies in two countries fell together in a single session, which is liquidation rather than analysis.

The bear case for the theme itself

Argued at full strength: memory is the most reliably cyclical industry in technology and every cycle in its history has ended the same way — producers add capacity into strength, supply arrives together, and prices fall much faster than they rose. All three major producers are currently investing. The bear case does not require demand to weaken at all; it only requires supply to arrive. Furthermore, a 5.3x forward multiple is not evidence that the market is wrong; it is the market's considered estimate that current earnings are unsustainable, and in this industry that estimate has usually been correct eventually. The question is only timing, and buying a cyclical because the multiple is low is one of the most reliable ways to lose money in this sector.

**This is why our Micron rating is Overweight at Medium conviction rather than High.** The bear case is strong enough that position sizing should be set against it.

ETF expressions

Memory has no clean listed pure-play vehicle outside single names — broad semiconductor ETFs dilute the exposure heavily and Korean listings require either ADRs or a country fund. This is a theme where the ETF route materially degrades the thesis, which is worth noting explicitly in the ETF portfolio work rather than forcing an approximation.
