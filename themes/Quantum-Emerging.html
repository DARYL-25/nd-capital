Quantum & Emerging Compute

Genuinely important if it arrives, genuinely unpredictable as to when. We cover it to watch it, not to own it.

Stage · Emerging — speculative

Quantum computing and other post-classical architectures would, at sufficient scale, change what is computationally possible in chemistry, materials and cryptography. Error correction has improved materially but commercially useful, fault-tolerant machines are not yet demonstrated, and the listed pure-plays are pre-revenue or near-pre-revenue with valuations driven entirely by narrative. We hold no position and cover the theme to monitor it.

Be honest about lateness

Where we are in the lifecycle

Be honest about lateness. Most money in thematic investing is lost by arriving at "Crowded."

Emerging and speculative. Periodic announcement-driven rallies in the listed pure-plays are characteristic of a field where progress is real but commercial timing is unknowable. This is the stage where the honest analytical answer is "we cannot value this," and our standards treat that as a valid conclusion rather than a failure.

Second- and third-order beneficiaries

This is where the non-obvious money is. Who supplies the supplier?

Post-quantum cryptography migration is the near-term, actually-investable consequence and it is largely independent of when quantum computers arrive — organisations must migrate against the possibility, and "harvest now, decrypt later" concerns are already driving procurement. That demand is real today, and it sits mostly in security vendors outside our coverage. The investable consequence of quantum computing is currently in security, not in computing.

What the market is rewarding right now

Announcements, which is a warning sign. When a theme's prices respond to technical milestones rather than to revenue, the prices are not carrying financial information.

The bear case for the theme itself

Argued at full strength: quantum computing has been roughly a decade away for three decades. Error correction overhead remains enormous, the algorithms with proven exponential advantage address a narrow set of problems, and classical hardware keeps improving on the same problems in the meantime. The listed pure-plays are largely narrative vehicles with minimal revenue and persistent dilution. Even if the technology arrives, the value may accrue to the large-cap research programmes that funded it from operating cash flow rather than to the pure-plays that funded it by issuing shares.
