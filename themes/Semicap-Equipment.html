Semiconductor Capital Equipment

The tools that build the fabs. Two capex cycles — logic and memory — are now overlapping in the same order books.

Stage · Accelerating

Semiconductor capital equipment vendors sell the lithography, etch, deposition and inspection systems that fabs require, which makes their order books an 18-to-24-month leading indicator for the entire industry. What is unusual right now is that the logic capex cycle driven by AI accelerators and the memory capex cycle driven by HBM are running simultaneously rather than in their usual offset. The three major US-listed vendors trade in a narrow band of 32–34x forward earnings despite very different exposure to the cycle that is actually accelerating.

Be honest about lateness

Where we are in the lifecycle

Be honest about lateness. Most money in thematic investing is lost by arriving at "Crowded."

Accelerating, with the important caveat that semicap is a derivative of a derivative — it depends on fab capex, which depends on end demand. That gives it more upside torque and considerably more downside torque than the chipmakers themselves.

Evidence for this staging: memory producers are reallocating and expanding capacity; logic capex remains elevated; and bookings have not yet turned. The single best staging indicator available is ASML's quarterly net bookings, which lead the whole complex.

Second- and third-order beneficiaries

This is where the non-obvious money is. Who supplies the supplier?

The service and spares annuity is the under-appreciated element across all four names. It grows with the installed base rather than with new orders, which means it keeps growing through a capex downturn. In a sector where the central risk is cycle timing, a revenue stream that is counter-cyclical to new-system sales materially changes the downside — and it is rarely valued separately.

What the market is rewarding right now

Memory leverage, though the market has not yet priced it. The three US names trade at 32–34x forward on growth rates of 11%, 15% and 30%. Either the market believes memory capex is about to stop, or it has not adjusted the relative multiples. Our ranking — LRCX > KLAC > AMAT — is the core output of this theme.

The bear case for the theme itself

Argued at full strength: semicap is the most cyclically amplified way to own semiconductors. Tools are ordered 18 months ahead of need, so if AI capex is cut in 2027 the order cancellations arrive before the revenue decline shows up anywhere else in the chain. These names are also at elevated absolute multiples — 32–36x forward for businesses that have historically traded at 15–20x through the cycle. An investor buying semicap here is making two bets simultaneously: that fab capex holds, and that the market continues to grant these businesses a structurally higher multiple than history. Both could be wrong at once, and they usually fail together.

ETF expressions

Semicap is captured within broad semiconductor ETFs but at low weights relative to the chipmakers. There is no dedicated listed vehicle with meaningful liquidity, so the theme is best expressed through the single names — which is convenient here, because the entire insight is a relative one within the group and an index would neutralise it.
