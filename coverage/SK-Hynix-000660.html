Our view

Investment thesis

SK hynix is the leading supplier of high-bandwidth memory, the component that gates how fast AI accelerators can be fed data and therefore one of the genuine physical constraints on the entire build-out. All 2026 HBM supply across the industry is reported sold out, and HBM contract pricing was raised roughly 20% for 2026. Against that, the shares fell more than 15% in a single session during the late-July selloff alongside Samsung — a move of a size that indicates forced de-risking rather than fundamental revision. Our view mirrors our Micron thesis: the operational data and the price action are pointing in opposite directions, and the data is currently more credible. We are publishing this without a price target, because our market-data vendor does not cover the Korean listing on our current plan and we will not manufacture a target from figures we cannot verify.

The edge, if there is one

What the market is missing

The same divergence as Micron, expressed in the market with the highest HBM share. DRAM contract prices are rising, HBM is sold out for the year, and the equities of the two largest producers fell more than 15% in a session. Three separately-listed companies in two countries do not simultaneously revise their fundamental outlooks in one trading day. That was liquidation.

Second, HBM leadership is not a commodity position. HBM requires customer qualification, which takes quarters and creates real switching costs — the economics sit between commodity DRAM and a specialty component. Investors applying a commodity-memory mental model to an HBM-weighted producer are using the wrong framework, and that mis-framing is the source of the opportunity.

Held to account

What would prove us wrong

Specific and monitorable. If any of these prints, we change the view in public.

Specific and monitorable. Agent A3 (Thesis Guardian) watches these. Vague conditions are useless.

  1. DRAM contract prices decline sequentially — the same trigger as our Micron thesis.
  2. A competitor achieves HBM4 qualification at a major customer at scale, eroding the leadership premium.
  3. Capacity expansion is announced materially above current plans by any of the three major producers.

HBM revenue as a share of DRAM revenue, HBM market share, DRAM and NAND ASPs, bit shipment growth, capex discipline, and the HBM sold-out horizon.

Expectations & earnings reaction pattern

High earnings risk with an added complication: as a Korean listing, this name is exposed to foreign-investor flow dynamics and currency that do not affect our US coverage. Those flows amplified the July selloff and are not something we currently model.

Momentum, positioning & narrative

Narrative read:

Sharply negative following a greater-than-15% single-session decline. We do not have the moving average series to quantify the trend structure properly, which is a real limitation on this name and one reason conviction is Medium rather than higher despite the strength of the fundamental argument.

Reminder: a stock with perfect fundamentals that the market has decided to dislike will not go up. Momentum is evidence, not noise.

Teaching note

The lesson here is about the integrity of publishing incomplete work. We have a genuine view on SK hynix, supported by verifiable industry data on HBM supply and pricing. What we do not have is reliable market data for the Korean listing. The disciplined response is to publish the thesis, publish the reasoning, and explicitly withhold the price target — rather than either dropping coverage (losing the insight) or fabricating a target from unverified numbers (losing the credibility).

The general principle: state your confidence separately for each component of an analysis. It is entirely coherent to be confident about an industry dynamic and uncertain about a valuation. Research that presents a single blended confidence level for everything it says is hiding information that the reader needs. Note also what this makes explicit — a data gap is an actionable finding.
